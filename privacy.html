Shorts Factory — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

What the app is

Shorts Factory is a private tool operated by a single person to create and upload original animated Shorts to their own YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

Data the app accesses

Shorts Factory uses YouTube API Services. With the channel owner's permission, granted through Google OAuth, it accesses:

  • youtube.upload: to upload videos, titles, descriptions and tags to the owner's channel.
  • youtube.readonly: to read the owner's channel and video details.
  • yt-analytics.readonly: to read the owner's channel performance metrics (views, watch time, retention).

It also reads public video statistics (titles, view, like and comment counts) through the YouTube Data API and public Google Trends data to find trending topics.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to research topics, publish the owner's videos and measure their performance.
  • OAuth tokens and collected statistics are stored locally on the owner's computer. They are not sold, shared or transferred to third parties.
  • No data about other users is collected, other than public statistics of public videos.
  • Stored API data is refreshed or deleted within 30 days, as required by the YouTube API Services policies.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops all uploads and analytics reads; local tokens can then be deleted.

Third-party terms

By using this app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
