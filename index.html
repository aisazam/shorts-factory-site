Shorts Factory

Shorts Factory is a private, single-user tool that produces original animated cartoon YouTube Shorts in English and Spanish and uploads them to its owner's own YouTube channel.

Developed with YouTube

How it uses YouTube API Services

  • YouTube Data API: reads public video statistics for trend research and uploads videos to the owner's channel.
  • YouTube Analytics API: reads the owner's own channel performance metrics.

Only the channel owner authorizes and uses the app. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Read our Privacy Policy to learn how data is handled, including the Google Privacy Policy.
